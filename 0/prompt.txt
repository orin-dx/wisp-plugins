### What a genuinely strong PR template includes (synthesized, not just "adequate")

- A **type-of-change signal** the author sets, not just prose (Kubernetes: label commands; simpler version: a checkbox list)
- **Why**, separate from what — the diff already shows what
- **Linked issue with auto-close convention** (`Fixes #N` / `Closes #N`)
- A **forced user-facing-impact decision** — Kubernetes forces either `NONE` or real release-note text in a dedicated block; can't be skipped silently
- **Reviewer-facing notes** section (context a reviewer needs that isn't in the diff)
- Optional space for docs/spec links
